Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don’t.

A focused client with the controls that matter — built on Media3/ExoPlayer, with quality, casting, downloads and Shorts done properly.

Playback tuned to your phone

Hardware-friendly H.264/AAC by default, with a quality picker from 144p to 2160p. Merge high-quality video and audio automatically, or pin the height you want — switching resumes exactly where you were.

ExoPlayer144p–2160p0.25×–4×Resume

Cast to the big screen

Find DLNA/UPnP renderers on your own Wi-Fi — a smart TV, a speaker, another phone running VLC or Kodi — and drive transport, seek and volume from the phone.

DLNAUPnPRemote

Truly offline

Keep a video on the device and watch it with no connection. Interrupted transfers resume from a part file, and a storage cap trims the oldest finished download first.

DownloadsResumeStorage cap

Shorts, done right

A vertical swipe feed with its own progress bar, tap to pause, hold for 2×, captions, and a rail with like, dislike and comment counts. The channel line opens the channel.

Swipe feed2× holdCC menu

A player built for watching

An in-player gear for quality, speed, captions, audio-only, background playback and SponsorBlock. Chapters from the description, a share link, an end screen, and honest dislike counts.

In-player settingsChaptersEnd screen

Your data stays yours

History, favourites, resume points and subscriptions live on the device. Back up everything to a single JSON file you choose where to put; sync is optional, off by default and pull-first.

The player

Thoughtful where it counts.

01

Quality you control

Auto prefers hardware-decodable H.264 up to 1080p, and still allows 1440p/2160p when nothing smaller exists. Pin any height the video offers.

02

Sound without the screen

Audio-only mode, background playback through a MediaSessionService, a real media notification and lock-screen controls. Hold the video for 2×.

03

Subtitles and chapters

The video’s own caption tracks with auto-translation and a fallback to the original, plus chapters detected from the description that seek on tap.

04

A window that keeps watching

Back docks the video into a mini-player over the feed; swipe home for the system picture-in-picture window. Drag it aside, tap to reopen, close to stop.

05

Quiet about the noise

SponsorBlock auto-skip for sponsor, self-promo and interaction segments, and Return YouTube Dislike counts — both optional.

Privacy

Private by construction.

Not a setting you have to find. The release dependency graph has no ad SDK and no analytics SDK, and your library never leaves the device unless you export it.

  • No ads, ad SDKs, trackers or telemetry.The release build ships none of them.
  • No account required.Sign-in is optional and runs in an in-app browser.
  • History, favourites and resume points stay local.Cookies live in private storage and are excluded from backups.
  • Backups are one JSON file you place yourself.Drive, a USB stick, another phone — your call.

Open Android

Android is open. Let’s keep it that way.

WeTube exists because Android lets you choose what runs on your own phone. That promise is under threat. Google plans to require every Android developer to register with it — pay a fee, accept its terms and hand over government ID — before an app can be installed on a certified device. Not just Play Store apps. Every app.

WeTube is small, independent, free and installed from a link rather than a store — exactly the software this change puts at risk. We stand with Keep Android Open.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is WeTube free?
Yes. It is free and open source under the MIT license, and there is no paid tier or subscription.
Do I need a Google account?
No. The app works without signing in. An account is optional and only adds your own feed and the channels you follow on YouTube.
Is it on the Play Store?
No. Every build is published as a GitHub release, and the CI artifacts are available too. The release APK is signed with the project key.
Does it show ads or track me?
No ads, no ad SDKs, no trackers, no analytics. The release dependency graph contains none of them, and history and preferences stay on the device unless you export a backup.
Which Android versions are supported?
Android 8.0 (API 26) and newer. The app targets Android 15 (API 35).
Is it affiliated with YouTube or Google?
No. WeTube is an independent, clean-room client. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to YouTube or Google.
How do I keep it up to date?
Install once and let Obtainium follow this repository’s releases for you — each new build appears with a tap. You can always re-download the APK from the latest release by hand instead.
